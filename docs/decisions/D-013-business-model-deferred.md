# D-013: Business model, open-source licensing and pricing are deferred

Status: **Accepted** by the owner on 2026-11-16, with the plan
([issue #1](https://github.com/guneet-xyz/shinan/issues/1),
[PR #2](https://github.com/guneet-xyz/shinan/pull/2)).

## Context

- Shinan's first user is the owner; small teams are a possible later audience
  "if it proves itself" ([plan, section 2](../plan.md#2-who-it-is-for)).

## Decision

Business model, open-source licensing and pricing are deferred until Shinan
has proved itself in our own use.

## Reasons

No evidence yet of demand outside our own use.

## Consequences

- **Decided:** building a business is not a goal of this phase
  ([plan, section 2](../plan.md#2-who-it-is-for)).
- **Decided:** the repository stays private for now
  ([D-011](D-011-private-repository-and-brand.md)).
- **Proposed:** documents do not rely on any particular pricing, licence or
  business model. Reason: none has been chosen, so a design that depends on one
  would rest on an assumption.
- **Open:** licensing if the project is ever made public
  ([plan, question 10](../plan.md#7-open-questions)).
- **Open:** what "proved itself" means. The readiness criteria for moving the
  `workspace` repository into Shinan ([D-018](D-018-start-empty-migrate-later.md))
  may inform it. Owned by [roadmap and migration](../roadmap.md).

## Alternatives considered

- **Choose a business model, licence and pricing now.** Rejected: no evidence
  of demand outside the owner's own use.

## Owning documents

- [Product overview](../product-overview.md): goals and non-goals.
- [Roadmap and migration](../roadmap.md): when this is revisited.
